Technology architecture

The project compares multiple scenarios, from minimum investment to a larger integrated renewable-hydrogen system.

1

Scenario A · Minimal Investment

PV-focused concept using roof and nearby available space to maximize direct self-consumption and reduce electricity imports.

2

Scenario B · Balanced System

PV plus battery storage to improve load matching, reduce peak import and create a more stable renewable supply profile.

3

Scenario C · Hydrogen Hub

PV, wind/PPA, battery and electrolyzer integration to produce green hydrogen and open commercial offtake opportunities.

Project roadmap

A structured development process from technical modeling to investor-ready recommendation.

Phase 1

Energy demand and renewable potential

Analyze hourly electricity demand, solar PV yield, wind potential and site constraints.

Phase 2

System sizing and component selection

Define PV capacity, battery capacity, electrolyzer size, hydrogen storage and safety-relevant components.

Phase 3

Financial and CO₂ evaluation

Calculate LCOE, LCOH, CAPEX, OPEX, annual cost balance, CO₂ savings and sensitivity cases.

Phase 4

Recommendation and pitch

Prepare a clear scenario recommendation, investor one-pager, business case and implementation plan.
