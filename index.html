The problem: clean energy decisions are becoming too complex.

Commercial and industrial sites are under pressure from energy costs, CO₂ targets and electricity market volatility. At the same time, technology options are expanding quickly.

€

Energy cost risk

High electricity demand turns energy purchasing into a strategic business risk, especially when spot markets are volatile.

CO₂

Regulatory pressure

Fit for 55, RED III, RFNBO rules and hydrogen strategies increase the pressure to prove credible decarbonization pathways.

⚡

Wrong use of surplus power

Renewable electricity should not automatically be exported. Without optimization, clean energy can be sold at weak prices or curtailed.

Our solution

A digital decision layer between renewable power, storage and hydrogen offtake.

Perpetua combines software-based analysis with professional consulting. The tool processes site data, load profiles, generation profiles, market prices, storage options, hydrogen pathways and regulatory assumptions.
