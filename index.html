The problem: clean energy decisions are becoming too complex.

Commercial and industrial sites are under pressure from energy costs, CO₂ targets and electricity market volatility. At the same time, technology options are expanding quickly.

€

Energy cost risk

High electricity demand turns energy purchasing into a strategic business risk, especially when spot markets are volatile and future prices are uncertain.

CO₂

Regulatory pressure

Fit for 55, RED III, RFNBO rules and hydrogen strategies increase the pressure to prove credible decarbonization pathways.

⚡

Wrong use of surplus power

Renewable electricity should not automatically be exported. Without optimization, clean energy can be sold at weak prices or curtailed.

Our solution

A digital decision layer between renewable power, storage, markets and hydrogen.

Perpetua combines software-based analysis with professional consulting. The tool processes site data, load profiles, generation profiles, market prices, storage options, hydrogen pathways and regulatory assumptions.

Product vision

A decision cockpit for clean-energy investments.

The platform translates technical and market complexity into an intuitive investment workflow: upload site data, compare scenarios, identify the best use of renewable electricity and export a decision-ready report.

Scenario builder Hourly optimization Investor reporting Portfolio scaling

Technology portfolio evaluated by Perpetua.

The company does not sell hardware. It evaluates which combination of generation, storage, hydrogen and market strategy makes economic sense for a specific site.

Photovoltaics

Evaluates roof, carport or open-area PV potential, hourly yield, self-consumption and investment impact.

Wind / PPA

Compares complementary renewable profiles and possible PPA structures to improve clean energy availability.

Battery storage

Sizes storage for load matching, peak reduction, higher self-consumption and lower exposure to volatile prices.

Electrolyzer

Checks when H₂ production is profitable, what size is suitable and how LCOH changes under scenarios.

H₂ storage & offtake

Assesses tanks, offtaker logic, industrial use cases and bankability of hydrogen value paths.

Fuel cell / backup

Considers future flexibility options such as backup power, mobility supply or site energy resilience.

Why now?

Market volatility and hydrogen policy create demand for smarter optimization.

More renewable capacity means more clean electricity, but also more fluctuation, surplus and flexibility needs. Perpetua sits at this interface: renewable generation, batteries, electricity markets and hydrogen economics.
